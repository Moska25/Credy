Subgroup diagnostics

Aggregate AUC hides where a model ranks badly or misprices risk. Performance and calibration by age band, employment type, region and channel on the test months, each with a bootstrap interval and its cohort size.

This is a diagnostic, not a fairness audit. The data is synthetic and no protected characteristic in the legal sense is modelled. Age band and employment type are produced by app/generate.py, and any gap you see below is a property of a generator, not evidence about any real lender. A genuine fairness assessment needs real outcomes, a defined protected attribute, a legal definition of the harm, and a review process that a website cannot stand in for.

How to read the intervals

Cohort size drives everything here. Compare the CI width column against the applications column: the largest levels have intervals a third the width of the smallest ones. Two levels whose intervals overlap are not distinguishable, however different their point estimates look. Before acting on any row below, ask whether the difference survives the interval, and whether you would have looked at that dimension at all if the number had come out the other way.
Multiple comparisons. This page reports {{ n_intervals }} bootstrap intervals. At 95% coverage you should expect roughly one in twenty to exclude the truth by chance alone, and no correction for that is applied here. Treat a single striking row as a hypothesis, not a result.
